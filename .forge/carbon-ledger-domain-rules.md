# Carbon Ledger domain rules

- Emissions calculations are high-risk.
- Activity data × emission factor calculations must be traceable.
- Emission factors retain source, publisher, year, geography, version and unit metadata.
- Scope 1, Scope 2 and Scope 3 must not be blurred.
- Location-based and market-based Scope 2 are labelled separately.
- Spend-based Scope 3 estimates are labelled as estimates; screening is not a quantified inventory.
- Locked reporting periods, frozen management packs and approved LCA versions must not be silently mutated.
- Approved activity records require versioning and audit history; recalculations create explicit audit events.
- Evidence supports claims but does not automatically prove implementation.
- Demo seed data is never presented as real company data.
- Supplier PCFs, assumptions and exclusions remain traceable.
- Role permissions, tenant boundaries and audit trails are high-risk.
